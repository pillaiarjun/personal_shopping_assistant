How Sasha works

Sasha is a live video stylist inside the Fashion Barn store. You talk to her over your mic and camera. She talks back, drives the store page on your screen, builds outfits from the catalog and shows you wearing them. This page maps every moving part.

One call, start to finish

What happens when you press "Talk to Sasha"

  1. Sign in to the call

    The widget asks the web server for a LiveKit token and joins a fresh call room with your camera and mic. The token carries your "Video face" choice.

  2. Sasha joins

    LiveKit sends the call to the agent worker on EasyPanel. It opens a Gemini 3.8 Live session with Sasha's script and the store tools. With the video face on, it also brings Tavus into the room, and Olivia's face speaks her lines; otherwise you hear her voice with her photo.

  3. She talks and looks

    Your voice and camera frames stream to Gemini Live, which answers in Sasha's voice. She sees what you wear or hold up.

  4. She drives the page

    When she calls a tool, the worker sends a page action over LiveKit's "barn" channel. The widget shows products, points, scrolls or opens the bag, and the call window shrinks into the sidebar.

  5. Outfits in seconds

    build_outfits uses a ready-made library look when one fits your occasion and budget (instant). Otherwise Flash-Lite builds three new outfits live, each 4 to 6 pieces with an accessory and within budget.

  6. You, wearing it

    While you look at the outfits, Nano Banana 2 dresses your camera snapshot in each one. The picture is ready by the time you pick.

  7. Into the bag

    She asks your sizes, adds the pieces to the store's bag and walks you to checkout.

Her tools

What Sasha can do in the store

search_products

Finds items by meaning ("something in linen") and shows them.

show_new_arrivals

Puts the latest pieces on screen.

show_product

Opens one product and walks through its photos with her pointer.

point_at

Moves her cursor to a part of the page, and can click it.

scroll_page

Scrolls you to a section.

build_outfits

Three complete outfits for the occasion and budget, or built around a piece you hold up.

pick_outfit

Opens the outfit you chose, piece by piece.

show_on_me

Reveals the try-on picture of you in it.

swap_piece

Trades one piece for another and updates the total.

add_to_bag

Adds a piece in your size to the bag.

Under the hood

Details that keep calls smooth

Video face switch

A "Video face" switch above "Talk to Sasha" turns the Tavus face on for that call. It starts off (voice only); the server's AVATAR setting changes the default, with no code change. Your browser remembers your choice.

Quiet-timer

If the line goes quiet when it's Sasha's turn, the worker nudges Gemini to keep going. After she asks a question it waits longer, so you have time to think.

Build around your piece

Hold up a shirt and every outfit keeps it. The try-on copies it from the camera frame taken while you showed it.

Budget guard

Each outfit's total is checked against your budget. Pieces that run a little over get swapped for cheaper ones in the same slot.

Occasion check

The builder works out how dressy the event is from the catalog and keeps clearly-too-casual clothes out.

One worker

Everything runs in one EasyPanel container: the web server and the agent worker. Each push to the v2 branch is redeployed from the Dockerfile.

Call logs

Each call writes a line-by-line log (what was said, tools, nudges) for reviewing glitches afterwards.
